Write these sections, in this order, each one short, and leave one out only when it has nothing:

1. **Goal**: what the agent was asked to do, in its brief and since.
2. **Constraints and preferences**: what the person or the parent agent asked for or ruled out.
3. **Done, in progress, blocked**: each item with where it stands.
4. **Key decisions**: each with its reason.
5. **Next steps**: what the agent was about to do, in order.
6. **Files**: the files read and the files changed, with their exact paths.
7. **Errors and fixes**: each error met, its exact message, and how it was fixed or why it was not.
8. **Critical context**: anything else the agent cannot go on without.
9. **What Flottille holds**: the agent's brief, its children and their state, its subscriptions,
   its pending requests and deliveries, its workspace and the git state of its branches, as the
   Flottille state block says them.

Keep exact paths, names, numbers, commands and error messages as they were written. Leave out
greetings, thinking aloud and anything repeated.
